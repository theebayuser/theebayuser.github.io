Choosing

What I animate

I try to animate concepts that either (1) are hard to understand without a visual aid, (2) lack concise and accurate visualizations, or (3) may be aesthetically pleasing. In rare cases, all three!

Process

How I animate

Once I find a satisfactory topic, I'll plan out the video and its scenes on paper. I'll render the animation with either the Manim library or MATLAB, then add the final touches and edit in DaVinci Resolve.

Check it yourself

Plate II, in your hands

A film can only assert that the helix lands on −1. Here the same claim is yours to move: drag the point around the unit circle and read the two components off the axes. At θ = π the imaginary part is gone and what is left is −1, exactly.

θ = 3.1416 rad = 180.0°     e^(iθ) = −1.0000 + 0.0000 i
