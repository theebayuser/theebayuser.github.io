How they get made

Storyboard, then cut it in half

It starts on paper. If I cannot draw the key frame by hand, no animation will save it. Then the scene goes into Python, usually far too long on the first pass, and the real work is deciding what to throw away.

The Lorenz attractor on the left is a small example, drawn live by this page rather than embedded: three coupled equations integrated one small step at a time, tracing a single unbroken line that never quite closes.

The one rule

Animate what moves, hold the rest

Motion is emphasis, so spending it on decoration wastes it. The double pendulum beside this is the rule in practice. Its finished trace is a tangle you could not read if I posted it as a still; watched, it is two arms under gravity, and you can see the exact moment the second one goes over the top and the whole path changes character.
