school · city, state · class of year

Abstract. I study infinite words over finite alphabets — which patterns are forced to appear, and which can be avoided forever — and then rebuild the proofs in Lean 4 until a computer agrees with every step. Alongside the research I write animations that make the arguments watchable, and I keep a public record of what worked, what failed, and why.

Keywords: pattern avoidance · repetition · formal verification · Mathlib · Manim

Selected work

Three things I would want you to look at first. Each is live work, not a class project — the write-ups and code go as deep as you care to follow.

Theorem 1 (a repetition threshold, resolved). ⋯ manuscript in preparation

An exact answer to a counting question about how long a word can go while avoiding a prescribed kind of repetition, for every alphabet past a small explicit size. Computer-assisted, independently reproduced from scratch, and being verified in Lean.

Lemma 2 (Thue, 1912 — machine-checked). ✓ machine-checked

The classical overlap-free and square-free theorems, formalized end to end in Lean 4 with no gaps left open. They anchor a small library of definitions for words, morphisms, and repetitions that the rest of my research is built on.
