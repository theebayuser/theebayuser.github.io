§ 01

Selected work

Three things I would want you to look at first. Each is live work, not a class project, and the write-ups go as deep as you care to follow.

Theorem 1 (a repetition threshold, resolved). ⋯ manuscript in preparation

An exact answer to a counting question about how long a word can go while avoiding a prescribed kind of repetition, for every alphabet past a small explicit size. Computer-assisted, independently reproduced from scratch, and being verified in Lean.

combinatorics on words·search + proof→
Lemma 2 (Thue, 1912, machine-checked). ✓ machine-checked

The classical overlap-free and square-free theorems, formalized end to end in Lean 4 with no gaps left open. They anchor a small library of definitions for words, morphisms, and repetitions that the rest of my research is built on.

Lean 4 + Mathlib·sorry-free→
Figure 3 (ideas in motion). ∎ ongoing

Short animated explanations built in Manim: the same arguments as the papers, drawn rather than written. Making a proof move is the fastest way to find the step I did not actually understand.
