A few honest things

Most of my week is arguing with a compiler that is always right, which has made me much harder to bluff and slightly worse at conversation. The part I like best is not solving something, it is the hour afterwards when I finally understand why it was true.

Outside that: I run a math club and an FBLA chapter, which taught me that organizing 250 people is a genuinely different skill from proving anything, and I make animations because watching a good one is what got me into this in the first place. I am still much better at starting things than finishing them, and I own too many half-read books about knots.
