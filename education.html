Where the math came from

schooling, official and otherwise

Nobody handed me this subject. The official curriculum got me through arithmetic and calculus, and everything past that came from contests, textbooks read out of order, and a proof assistant that refused to agree with me.

The figure is the honest version of my prerequisite chart. Two chains, one from competition mathematics into combinatorics on words, one from logic into Lean, meeting at the point where a theorem has to be typed out in full.
